What Members Receive

Official Hallmark Certification

Eligibility for BAJUS-supervised hallmark testing and certification of your products.

02

Daily Official Gold Price

Direct access to the daily BAJUS-issued gold and silver price for transparent retail trade.

03

Legal & Policy Support

Representation in regulatory matters and access to the BAJUS legal cell for trade disputes.

04

Training & Skill Development

Subsidised participation in BAJUS workshops on design, gemology, and modern manufacturing.

05

Priority enrolment in domestic exhibitions and BAJUS-led delegations to international fairs.

06

Member Directory Listing

Your shop listed in the official BAJUS directory of certified jewellers across Bangladesh.

Eligibility

  • ● Valid trade licence issued in Bangladesh, currently active.
  • ● Valid dealing licence where applicable (for gold/silver dealers).
  • ● National Identification (NID) and Tax Identification Number (TIN).
  • ● VAT/BIN registration for the shop or manufacturing unit.
  • ● Commitment to the BAJUS code of conduct and hallmark standards.

Application Process

  1. 1

    Create Account

    Register online with your mobile number and create a secure password.

  2. 2

    Submit Documents

    Upload your NID, TIN, trade licence, VAT registration, and shop details through the online portal.

  3. 3

    Verification

    BAJUS Secretariat verifies your application with the relevant district committee.

  4. 4

    Pay Membership Fee

    Complete payment via Mobile Financial Service or bank transfer. Receive your official Membership ID.
