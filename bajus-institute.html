Training & Course Outline

Technical Training

Gold Assaying & Hallmarking Standards

Learn gold purity validation, fire assaying, chemical testing, and X-ray Fluorescence (XRF) spectrometry. Students gain hands-on training to operate hallmark scanning centers.

Instructor: Ranjit Ghosh
Details
Heritage Design

Traditional Filigree & Handmade Crafting

Master the ancient techniques of fine wire ornament creation, gold engraving, and gem setting. This course preserves traditional Bengali gold craftsmanship and hand tools operations.

Instructor: Swapan Karmakar
Details
Modern Technology

CAD Modelling & 3D Jewellery Printing

Bridge art and technology. Learn RhinoGold, Matrix, and CAD software to design digital ornaments, export STL files, and operate high-precision 3D wax printers.

Instructor: Kazi Sharmin
