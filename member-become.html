Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

Any registered jewellery business operating in Bangladesh is eligible to apply for BAJUS membership.

  • Registered gold/jewellery retail or wholesale business
  • Valid trade license from the relevant local authority
  • Proprietor/owner with valid NID and photo
  • Commitment to comply with BAJUS rules and code of conduct

How to Apply

Application Process

1
Gather Documents
Prepare your NID, trade license, TIN certificate, dealing license (if applicable), VAT/BIN registration, and a passport-sized photograph of the business owner.
2
Complete the Application Form
Fill in the BAJUS membership application form online or download it from our website. Provide accurate information about yourself and your business.
3
Submit Your Application
Submit the completed form and supporting documents online, or deliver them in person to the nearest BAJUS divisional office or the Dhaka head office.
4
Pay the Membership Fee
Once your application is reviewed, you will receive payment instructions. Pay via bank transfer, MFS (bKash/Nagad), or in person at the BAJUS office.
5
Receive Your Certificate
Upon approval and payment confirmation, your BAJUS membership certificate and ID card will be issued within 7 working days.
