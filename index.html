Founded in 1972, the Bangladesh Jewellers' Association (BAJUS) represents over 40,000 jewellers and manufacturers. As the industry's authoritative voice, we are dedicated to safeguarding ethical practices and modernising local manufacturing.

We believe in the power of jewellery to tell stories and celebrate traditions. Through unified standards and ethically sourced metals, our mission is to position Bangladesh as a premier exporter of fine craftsmanship.

Our mission is to transform the jewellery industry of Bangladesh into a globally competitive, ethical, and modern powerhouse — built on the trust of every customer and every craftsperson.

Under the new leadership, BAJUS is embarking on a journey of complete modernisation — laboratory-tested hallmarking, transparent daily pricing, and stronger protection for both jewellers and consumers across the country.
