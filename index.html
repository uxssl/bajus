About the Association

The Apex Body of Bangladesh's Jewellery Industry

Founded in 1972, the Bangladesh Jewellers' Association (BAJUS) is the national trade body representing the interests of over 40,000 jewellers, manufacturers, and traders across the country. We serve as the authoritative voice of the industry in policy, regulation, and international engagement.

Our mandate is to safeguard ethical practices, modernise the local jewellery manufacturing process, and position Bangladesh as a premier exporter of fine craftsmanship through unified standards and continuous advocacy.

"Our mission is to transform the jewellery industry of Bangladesh into a globally competitive, ethical, and modern powerhouse — built on the trust of every customer and every craftsperson."

Under the new leadership, BAJUS is embarking on a journey of complete modernisation — laboratory-tested hallmarking, transparent daily pricing, and stronger protection for both jewellers and consumers across the country.
