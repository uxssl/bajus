Standing Committees

BAJUS operates through specialised standing committees that oversee critical areas of work, ensure member representation, and drive the association's policy and advocacy agenda. Each committee reports to the Executive Board and is chaired by an elected Board member.

Banking And Financial Service
Banking, loans, and financial affairs

Oversees matters related to banking policies, financial services, and loan accessibility for BAJUS members to foster industry growth.

Daily price fixing, market surveillance

  1. Meeting 12 Jan 2025 · 11:00 AM

    Monthly committee meeting held at BAJUS Secretariat, Dhaka, to review gold and silver rate trends over the past month and finalize the daily pricing formula used for official rate announcements.

    • BAJUS Secretariat, Dhaka
    • 9 committee members attended
    • Chaired by Dewan Aminul Islam
    • Revised pricing formula approved for Q1
  2. Market Visit 02 Feb 2025 · 10:30 AM

    On-site visit to Tanti Bazar and Bashundhara City jewellery markets to observe retail pricing practices and gather feedback from shop owners on rate implementation.

    • Tanti Bazar & Bashundhara City, Dhaka
    • 5 members, 2 secretariat staff
    • 18 retail shops visited
    • Feedback report submitted to Executive Board
  3. Price Monitoring Daily · 9:00 AM

    Continuous daily tracking of international bullion rates, USD/BDT exchange movement, and regional gold exchange data to set the official local gold and silver price notice.

    • BAJUS Rate Desk, Secretariat
    • LBMA & regional exchange data sources
    • Handled by the Rate Monitoring Cell
    • Daily rate notice published by 10:00 AM
  4. Inspection 18 Mar 2025 · 3:00 PM

    Surprise inspection drive across member shops in Old Dhaka to verify compliance with the published daily price list and proper rate display.

    • Old Dhaka jewellery cluster
    • 3-member inspection team
    • 12 shops inspected
    • 2 shops issued verbal warning for outdated rate boards
  5. Enforcement-related Activities 05 Apr 2025 · 2:00 PM

    Review meeting to issue formal warnings and disciplinary notices against members found overcharging or violating the committee's pricing guidelines.

    • BAJUS Secretariat, Dhaka
    • Joint session with Law & Membership Committee
    • 3 cases reviewed
    • 1 formal warning issued, 1 case referred for further action
  6. Awareness Program 22 May 2025 · 4:00 PM

    Public awareness campaign at the BAJUS auditorium explaining how the daily gold rate notice is calculated and how consumers can verify fair pricing.

    • BAJUS Auditorium, Dhaka
    • 60+ retail members and press attended
    • Presented by the Pricing Committee Chairman
    • Rate calculation guide distributed to attendees
  7. Seminar / Workshop 14 Jun 2025 · 10:00 AM

    Workshop for BAJUS members on reading international bullion market signals and understanding their impact on local gold and silver pricing.

    • BAJUS Conference Hall, Dhaka
    • 40 members attended
    • Led by a senior bullion market analyst
    • Training certificates issued to participants
