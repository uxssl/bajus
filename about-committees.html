Standing Committees

BAJUS operates through specialised standing committees that oversee critical areas of work, ensure member representation, and drive the association's policy and advocacy agenda. Each committee reports to the Executive Board and is chaired by an elected Board member.

Banking And Financial Service
Banking, loans, and financial affairs

Oversees matters related to banking policies, financial services, and loan accessibility for BAJUS members to foster industry growth.

Pricing and Price Monitoring
Daily price fixing, market surveillance

Convenes regularly to determine and announce official gold and silver prices for the Bangladesh market based on international market data.

Foreign Trade and Market Development
Export, international trade

Advises on export opportunities, international market development, and strengthening Bangladesh jewellery presence globally.

Tariff and Taxation
Fair taxation frameworks, customs duties

Engages with the National Board of Revenue to advocate for fair taxation, VAT policies, and customs duties for the jewellery sector.

Women Affairs
Women entrepreneurs, workplace equality

Supports women entrepreneurs and professionals in the jewellery sector, addressing their specific needs and promoting equality.

Anti-Smuggling and Law Enforcement
Anti-smuggling, law enforcement coordination

Collaborates with law enforcement agencies to combat gold smuggling, theft, and ensure a secure environment for jewellers.

Research and Development
Industry research, market analysis

Conducts research on consumer trends, market demands, and technological advancements to guide the industry's strategic direction.

Exhibition, Trade and Event Management
Trade fairs, BAJUS events

Organizes major trade events, jewellery exhibitions, and the BAJUS Annual Conference to showcase local craftsmanship.

Law and Membership
Legal affairs, membership registration

Oversees legal matters, constitutional amendments, dispute resolution, and member registration and welfare schemes.
