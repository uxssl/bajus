About BAJUS

Bangladesh Jewellers Association (BAJUS) is a non-political, non-profitable, and service-oriented traditional trade organization registered under the Companies Act of 1913 and licensed by the Ministry of Commerce. We are a first-class active member of the Federation of Bangladesh Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FBCCI).

Although the Bangladesh Jeweller’s Association started its activities to transform the post-independence war-torn country into the golden Bengal of dreams, with the aim of expanding the domestic gold industry and economic emancipation, it overcame various adversities and became institutionalized on Thursday 12th May 1984.

Since its inception, the main objective of Bangladesh Jeweller’s Association has been to ensure customer service, patronize the domestic gold industry and safeguard the interests of jewellery traders. To this end, BAJUS has district committees in every district of the country, which are represented by the Central Committee. At present Bangladesh Jeweller’s Association has about 18 (eighteen) thousand members.

BAJUS determines the price of gold in the domestic market, taking into account the international market, the domestic market and the general consumer. We coordinate with various government ministries and agencies, outlining the development of the jewellery industry and its significant contribution to the national economy, and promote rules and regulations among the members for proper and competitive business expansion.

There is a huge demand for Bangladeshi jewellery exports to developed countries like Europe, the Middle East, America, and Canada. Any handmade product is highly valued in the world market. The goldsmiths of Bangladesh make jewellery very meticulously which is not possible on machines, meaning our handmade jewellery is uniquely sought after. With preferential trade facilities (GSP) in European countries, there is a potential to export jewellery worth around Tk 7,000 crore.

Mission & Vision

Our guiding principles that shape every decision and initiative at BAJUS.

Our Mission

To organise, represent, and uplift the jewellery industry of Bangladesh by enforcing ethical standards, advocating fair regulation, and equipping members with the tools they need to thrive in a competitive global market.

Our Vision

A transparent, world-class Bangladeshi jewellery industry — recognised internationally for craftsmanship, integrity, and innovation, and trusted absolutely by every customer who purchases a piece carrying our hallmark.

Our Values

  • Integrity in every transaction — purity verified, weight honest.
  • Unity across districts, generations, and trade categories.
  • Advocacy that puts the long-term health of the industry first.
  • Modernisation through technology, training, and certification.

Our History

From a small assembly of Dhaka jewellers in 1972 to the national apex body of an industry serving millions.

1972

Foundation

Bangladesh Jewellers' Association is founded in newly independent Bangladesh as the first organised trade body for the jewellery sector.

1995

Nationwide Expansion

Establishment of district-level units extends BAJUS representation across all 64 districts of Bangladesh.

2018

First National Gold Policy

BAJUS advocacy leads to the adoption of Bangladesh's first formal gold import and trade policy, creating legitimate channels for the industry.

2025

A New Executive Board

The newly elected 2025–2027 Executive Board, led by President Enamul Haque Khan, takes office to lead the next phase of modernisation and hallmarking enforcement.

Milestones

Key Achievements

Landmark outcomes that have shaped the credibility, reach, and influence of BAJUS over five decades of service to the jewellery trade.

National Rate Authority

Established as the reference body for daily gold and silver pricing across Bangladesh, balancing international bullion markets with domestic consumer protection.

Nationwide Representation

Built district committees in all 64 districts with upazila-level outreach, uniting thousands of jewellers under one national umbrella.

Gold Policy Reform

Played a leading advocacy role in securing Bangladesh's first comprehensive national gold import and trade policy in 2018.

FBCCI Membership

Recognised as a first-class member of FBCCI, ensuring the jewellery sector has a formal seat at the table in national commerce and economic planning.

Skills & Certification

Launched the BAJUS Institute to train goldsmiths in assaying, hallmarking, filigree craft, and modern CAD design — preserving heritage skills while building export readiness.

Export Potential Realised

Championed Bangladeshi handmade jewellery on the world stage, highlighting export potential worth thousands of crores through GSP-linked markets in Europe, the Middle East, and North America.

Present Day

Major Activities

Core programmes and services BAJUS delivers today to members, consumers, and government partners across the country.

  • Daily Gold & Silver Rate Publication Issuing official market rates each business day for 22K, 21K, 18K gold and silver categories — displayed publicly and online for consumer transparency.
  • Member Registration & Directory Services Maintaining a verified national member directory, renewal processes, and shop-level accreditation for registered BAJUS outlets.
  • Government Liaison & Policy Advocacy Coordinating with the Ministry of Commerce, NBR, Bangladesh Bank, and other agencies on import duty, taxation, hallmarking rules, and industry development.
  • Consumer Protection & Ethical Trade Promoting fair weighing, purity standards, and dispute resolution mechanisms to build lasting trust between jewellers and the buying public.
  • Training & Institute Programmes Running courses in gold assaying, traditional craftsmanship, gemmology, and CAD design through the BAJUS Institute skills academy.
  • Trade Fairs, Exhibitions & BAJUS Day Organising industry gatherings, anniversary celebrations, and expo participation to showcase Bangladeshi jewellery domestically and abroad.
  • Hallmarking & Assaying Standards Advancing laboratory-tested hallmark enforcement and assaying compliance to align domestic practice with international quality benchmarks.
  • Industry Modernisation & Digital Services Deploying member portals, online notices, digital payment channels, and public information platforms to bring the trade into the modern era.
